If you signed a utility solar PPA in 2024, you almost certainly received **p-type PERC** modules. If you're signing one in 2026, **n-type TOPCon** is now the default — and there's a good reason.

## The Numbers

| Metric | P-Type PERC | **N-Type TOPCon** |
|---|---|---|
| Cell Efficiency | ~22% | **26%+** |
| First-Year Degradation | ~2% | **1%** |
| Temperature Coefficient | -0.35%/°C | **-0.29%/°C** |
| Bifacial Gain | 5-10% | **15-25%** |
| 25-Year Output | 84.8% | **89.4%** |

## What This Means for Your LCOE

For a 100 MW utility project in West Texas, switching from PERC to TOPCon typically reduces LCOE by **$2–4/MWh** over the 25-year project life. On a 25-year, 4 MWh/MW capacity basis, that's millions in additional revenue.
