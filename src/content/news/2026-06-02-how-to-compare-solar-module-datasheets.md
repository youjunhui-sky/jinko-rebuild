A datasheet is more than a specification table. For distributors, dealers and EPC installers, it is often the first document used to compare product fit, warranty confidence and sales readiness.

## What to compare first

- Module wattage and power tolerance
- Cell technology and module structure
- Temperature coefficient and low-light notes
- Mechanical dimensions and weight
- Warranty and certification references
- Packaging, MOQ and logistics notes

## Why this matters for B2B sales

When sales teams can quickly answer datasheet questions, they reduce back-and-forth during early project qualification. A structured product page should connect the datasheet to applications, recommended solutions and case references.
