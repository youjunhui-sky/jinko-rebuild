Commercial and industrial rooftop solar projects become easier to sell and deliver when installers work with repeatable product packages, clear specifications and proven project references.

Callsun is building a product and solution system around standard solar modules, grid-tied storage packages, off-grid packages and C&I case references so distributors and installers can reduce project design friction.

Key priorities for B2B partners:

- Stable product quality and warranty confidence
- Flexible supply for project schedules
- Repeatable BOMs for common system sizes
- Case references that match local customer scenarios
- Clear technical documentation and inquiry response
