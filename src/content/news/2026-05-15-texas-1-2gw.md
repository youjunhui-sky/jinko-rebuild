Jinko Solar today announced the completion of module deliveries for a **1.2 GW utility-scale solar project** in West Texas — the largest single-site deployment of Jinko's flagship **EAGLE® G7 TOPCon modules** to date.

## Project Highlights

- **Capacity**: 1,200 MW DC
- **Modules**: 2.1 million EAGLE® G7 575W panels
- **Origin**: 100% manufactured at Jinko's Jacksonville, FL facility
- **Commissioning**: Q3 2026
- **Grid**: ERCOT

The project qualifies for the **IRA Domestic Content Bonus Credit**, providing the developer an additional 10% in investment tax credits.

> "Jinko's ability to deliver Made-in-USA modules at gigawatt scale is exactly why we partnered with them," said the project's lead developer. "Their Jacksonville factory has been a game-changer for our timeline and our ITC qualification."

## About EAGLE® G7

EAGLE® G7 modules utilize n-type TOPCon cell technology with cell efficiencies exceeding 26%, delivering industry-leading power density and reliability for utility-scale deployments.
